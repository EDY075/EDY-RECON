# Acceptable Use

EDY RECON is intended for defensive research, education, audit, and penetration
testing on systems owned by the operator or covered by explicit written
authorization.

## Required conduct

- Define the target, time window, methods, and limits in writing.
- Use the minimum data and request volume necessary.
- Respect provider terms, rate limits, privacy law, and contractual duties.
- Keep credentials, reports, sessions, and local datasets private.
- Stop immediately if activity leaves the approved scope or risks harm.
- Preserve evidence and notify the responsible party through an approved path.

## Prohibited conduct

- Unauthorized access, scanning, credential testing, or surveillance.
- Credential stuffing, account takeover, harassment, or service disruption.
- Collection, publication, sale, or redistribution of personal data or leaked
  credentials without a lawful basis.
- Bypassing access controls, rate limits, monitoring, or provider restrictions.
- Using the project to conceal attribution, maintain persistence, or cause harm.
- Uploading real secrets or third-party data to issues, demonstrations, or AI
  providers without approval.

The software license does not grant permission to access third-party systems or
redistribute external datasets. Operators remain responsible for authorization,
scope, and compliance.
